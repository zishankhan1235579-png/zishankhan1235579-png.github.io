Diyaar, Kuwait · via Upwork

A quality management portal with approvals and live dashboards

The challengeThe client needed one secure place to manage quality management (QMS) documents across several companies. Every new or changed document had to be reviewed and approved, and managers had no easy way to see where requests stood.

What I builtA complete SharePoint portal with document libraries, a request and approval workflow, and dashboards for management. The portal also covers staff attendance and a current events hub.

  • SharePoint Online
  • Approval workflows
  • Custom dashboards
  • Document management

  1. One home for every team

    Staff land on a single home page with the document library for each company, a New Request button and their own pending tasks.

  2. Submit and track requests

    Anyone can raise a document request and follow it in one list, searchable by keyword, status and division. Open requests, uploads this week and average approval time sit right at the top.

  3. Review and approval, automatically

    Each request moves through review stages on its own. Reviewers see it in their pending tasks, and its status updates at every step, so nothing gets lost in email.

  4. Dashboards for management

    Managers see task volume, pending, rejected and approved requests, obsolete documents, and documents by type and division, both on the SharePoint dashboard page and in a custom-designed dashboard.

Appgenix · PowerApps

An ICT risk register app with built-in scoring and dashboards

The challengeThe client's IT team needed a consistent way to log technology risks, rate them the same way every time, and follow each one through review, instead of tracking them in spreadsheets.

What I builtA PowerApps canvas app where staff log a risk through a guided form, the app calculates its rating from a likelihood and impact matrix, and each person gets a dashboard of their risks, review dates and pending requests. It also supports light and dark mode.

  • PowerApps
  • Power Automate
  • SharePoint
  • Approval workflows

  1. Log a new risk in a guided form

    A two-step form (New Risk, then Roles) captures the description, causes, effects, key controls, actions and risk owner. Required fields are marked, and every rating field has a reference table one click away.

  2. Consistent scoring with a risk matrix

    Likelihood and impact are each scored 1 to 5, and the app multiplies them into a rating band from Low to Extreme. The colour-coded matrix opens right inside the form, so everyone rates risks the same way.

  3. A personal dashboard for every user

    Each user sees their risks by status, overdue review dates, pending requests by risk rating, where each request sits in the approval process, and how many were submitted in the last seven days.
